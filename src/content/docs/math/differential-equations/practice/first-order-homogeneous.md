$$
y^{\prime}=f\left(\frac{y}{x}\right),\quad a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}
$$

## Postupci rješavanja:

### Jednadžbe oblika $y^{\prime}=f\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)$

**1. Korak:** Supstituiramo
$$
z(x)= \frac{y}{x} 
$$
**2. Korak:**
$$
y(x)=z(x)x
$$
**3. Korak:** 
$$
y^{\prime}(x)=z^{\prime}(x)x+z
$$

## Zadatci za vježbu:

**1. Zadatak:** Riješite DJ $(x-y)ydx-x^2dy=0$

**2. Zadatak:** Riješite CZ $\begin{cases} xy^{\prime}-y=x tg \frac{y}{x} \\ y(1)=0 \end{cases}$

**3. Zadatak:** Riješite DJ $y^{\prime}(x-2\sqrt{xy})=y$

## Rješenja zadataka:

**1. Zadatak:** 
$$
\begin{cases} \frac{x}{y}=\ln|x|+x,\quad c\in\mathbb{R} \\ \frac{y}{x}=0 \end{cases}
$$

**2. Zadatak:** $\sin{\frac{y}{x}}=0$

**3. Zadatak:** 
$$
\ln\left|\frac{y}{x}\right|-4\sqrt{\frac{y}{x}}=\ln|x|+c,\quad c\in\mathbb{R}
$$
